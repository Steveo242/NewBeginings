# Bloody Seafood — game description

**Studio:** Steel Gaming
**Format:** 7×7 cluster-wins slot
**Theoretical RTP:** 96.70%
**Maximum win:** 10,000× the play amount

## Short description

Bloody Seafood is a 7×7 cluster slot set on the blood-slicked dock of a
harbour fish market. Symbols pay in clusters rather than on lines: five or
more matching symbols touching horizontally or vertically form a win.

Winning symbols are cleared, everything above them falls, and fresh symbols
drop in to fill the gaps. If the new arrangement forms another cluster it
pays as well, and the sequence repeats until no new clusters appear. A
running multiplier is applied to cluster wins, and every win from a single
spin is totalled together.

Three or more scatters award free spins — 8 spins for three, rising to 18
for seven. A wild anchor joins the board during free spins and substitutes
for any paying symbol to complete a cluster. Landing three or more scatters
during the feature re-triggers it, adding up to 10 further spins.

Five play modes are offered: Base, two Ante modes that improve the chance
of reaching the feature, and two modes that open free spins immediately.

## One-line version

A 7×7 cluster slot on a harbour fish-market dock, where winning catches
clear away, cascade into fresh clusters and build a running multiplier —
with a free spins feature that adds a wild anchor to the board.
