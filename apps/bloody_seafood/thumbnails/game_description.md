# Bloody Seafood — game description

**Studio:** Steel Gaming
**Format:** 7×7 cluster-wins slot
**Theoretical RTP:** 96.70%
**Maximum win:** 10,000× the play amount

## Short description

Bloody Seafood is a 7×7 cluster slot set on the blood-slicked dock of a
harbour fish market. Symbols pay in clusters rather than on lines: five or
more matching symbols touching horizontally or vertically form a win.

Winning symbols are cleared, everything above them falls, and fresh symbols
drop in to fill the gaps. If the new arrangement forms another cluster it
wins as well, and the sequence repeats until no new clusters appear. Every
win from a single spin is totalled together.

Three or more scatters award free spins — 8 spins for three, rising to 20
for eight — and landing three or more again during the feature re-triggers
it for further spins. A wild anchor joins the board for free spins and
substitutes for any paying symbol to complete a cluster.

The bonus carries a global multiplier that applies to every cluster win. It
begins at 1× and climbs by 1 for each winning cluster containing a wild,
holding its value for the whole feature. There is no multiplier in the base
game.

Play Super goes further: wilds carry from one free spin to the next, up to
six at a time, redrawn into new positions each spin so the board keeps
shifting beneath them.

Five play modes are offered — Base, two Ante modes that improve the chance
of reaching the feature, and two that open free spins immediately.

## One-line version

A 7×7 cluster slot on a harbour fish-market dock, where winning catches
clear away and cascade into fresh clusters, and every wild caught in a win
drives a multiplier that builds across the whole bonus.
